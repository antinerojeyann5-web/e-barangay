🏛️ Welcome to e-Barangay

Your digital portal for barangay services, records, and community updates.

📖 Rules, Regulations & How to Use This System

Welcome, ka-barangay! Before you proceed, please read the following guidelines carefully. By using this system, you agree to follow these rules.

📋 Step-by-Step Guide: How to Use e-Barangay
  1. Register an Account: Click the "Register" button to create your personal resident account using your username and email.
  2. Log In: Use your username and password to securely log in to the system.
  3. Access Your Dashboard: After logging in, you will be redirected to your personal dashboard where you can see your information and requests.
  4. Request Documents: Go to your Dashboard and click "+ Request a New Document". Select the type of document you need, type your purpose, and submit.
  5. Submit Complaints: On your Dashboard, click "+ Submit a Complaint" to report any concerns or issues in the barangay.
  6. Track Your Requests: View the real-time status of your document requests on your dashboard.
  7. Read Announcements: Visit the "Announcements" page to stay updated with the latest news from the Barangay Hall.
⚖️ Rules and Regulations
  • Only registered residents of this barangay are allowed to create an account and use this system.
  • You must provide accurate and truthful information in all your requests, complaints, and profile details.
  • Do not submit false or malicious complaints. Filing false reports is punishable by law.
  • Your personal data is strictly protected under the Data Privacy Act of 2012 (Republic Act No. 10173).
  • Document requests may take 1 to 3 working days to process.
  • Approved documents must be picked up personally at the Barangay Hall during office hours.
  • Misuse of this system may result in account suspension and legal action.
